DSCR Calculator

Will the property cover its own mortgage?

The debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) compares a property’s net operating income to its annual mortgage payments. Lenders use it to decide whether the property generates enough income to service its debt.

A DSCR above 1.0 means the property covers its mortgage from operations. Most lenders want a cushion above that.

Formula

DSCR = Net operating income (NOI) ÷ Annual debt service

Annual debt service is 12 monthly principal-and-interest payments. It does not include taxes and insurance if those are escrowed separately and already counted in operating expenses.

How to calculate it

  1. Compute NOI from income and operating expenses.
  2. Compute the annual mortgage payment (principal and interest).
  3. Divide NOI by that annual payment.

FAQ

What DSCR do lenders require?

Many DSCR lenders look for 1.20–1.25 or higher. Some allow 1.0 for strong borrowers or lower-LTV loans.

What does a DSCR below 1 mean?

The property’s operations do not cover its mortgage; you would need to fund the shortfall from other income.

How can I improve DSCR?

Increase rent, reduce operating expenses, put more money down (lower loan), or refinance at a lower rate.
