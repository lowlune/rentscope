FAQ

Do I need Excel or Google Sheets?

No. RentScope Pro is a single self-contained HTML file that runs offline in any modern browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your inputs never leave your computer.

Does it handle short-term rentals?

The cash-flow and return math is the same for short- and long-term rentals — enter the revenue and expense assumptions that match your strategy.

Can I use it for commercial property?

It models single-property cash flow, financing and after-tax returns. Commercial deals often need additional assumptions (leases, tenant improvements, capex schedules) that are outside this model.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Free updates to the same download — re-download from your purchase receipt.
