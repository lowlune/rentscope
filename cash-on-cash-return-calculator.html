Cash-on-Cash Return Calculator

The return on the money you actually invested.

Cash-on-cash return measures annual pre-tax cash flow against the cash you put into the deal — down payment, closing costs and rehab. It answers a simple question: for every dollar I invest, how many dollars come back each year?

Unlike cap rate, cash-on-cash return includes the effect of leverage, so it reflects your actual equity position.

Formula

Cash-on-cash return = Annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ Total cash invested

Total cash invested includes the down payment, closing costs, and any upfront renovation. Annual pre-tax cash flow is NOI minus debt service.

How to calculate it

  1. Add up all cash you bring to closing.
  2. Compute annual cash flow after the mortgage.
  3. Divide annual cash flow by total cash invested.

FAQ

What is a good cash-on-cash return?

Many investors target 8% or higher, but leveraged deals in high-appreciation markets often accept less in exchange for equity growth.

Is cash-on-cash the same as ROI?

No. Cash-on-cash looks only at annual cash flow. ROI or IRR also account for appreciation and loan paydown over the holding period.

Does cash-on-cash include taxes?

Cash-on-cash is typically pre-tax. RentScope Pro adds after-tax cash-on-cash using depreciation and your marginal tax rate.
