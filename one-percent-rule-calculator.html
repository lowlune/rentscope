1% Rule Calculator

A fast screen, not a full analysis.

The 1% rule says monthly rent should be at least 1% of the purchase price. It is a quick filter to avoid wasting time on deals that are obviously too expensive — not a substitute for a real analysis.

Markets with high taxes, insurance or HOA dues often need more than 1%; low-cost markets may work below 1%.

Formula

1% rule ratio = Monthly rent ÷ Purchase price (expressed as a percentage)

Some investors use the 0.8% rule in expensive markets or the 2% rule in very low-cost markets. The right threshold depends on your expenses and financing.

How to calculate it

  1. Take the realistic monthly rent.
  2. Divide by the purchase price.
  3. Compare the result to 1% (or your market’s threshold), then run a full analysis.

FAQ

Is the 1% rule still relevant?

It remains a useful first screen in moderate-cost markets, but rising rates and insurance costs mean many deals need more than 1% to cash flow.

What is the 2% rule?

A stricter screen used in low-cost markets. Finding 2% deals is rare in most metropolitan areas.

What should I check after the 1% rule?

Run cash flow, cap rate, DSCR and a multi-year projection before making an offer.
